Problem Statement & Proposed Innovation

Transforming high-latency manual GIS mapping into instant, certified all-weather intelligence

Assam Flood Case Study Deterministic Math Contract
🚨 Problem Hook & Scope

"During Assam's monsoon floods, NDRF commanders cannot get a fast, certified answer to 'how much land is underwater?' Manual assessment in QGIS takes ~4 hours, with clouds blocking optical satellites roughly 78% of the time."

  • Keyword Deconstruction: Multimodal (SAR + Optical), Remote Sensing (CRS & GSD affine math), Assistant (Plain-English queries).
  • In-Scope Boundaries: Dual-pol SAR (RISAT/S1), Optical (Cartosat/S2), SRTM/CartoDEM slope gating, RFC 7946 GeoJSON.
  • Out-of-Scope Safeguards: Streaming uncalibrated drone video, LiDAR point clouds, black-box LLM area guesses.
🛰️ Proposed Solution
  • All-Weather Satellite Fusion: Fuses optical with C-band radar; microwave pulses (5.35 GHz) penetrate dense cloud decks and rain bands day and night.
  • Deterministic Area Guard: $\text{Area (km}^2\text{)} = \frac{N_{\text{pixels}} \times \text{GSD}_x \times \text{GSD}_y}{1,000,000}$, computed directly from GeoTIFF metadata—never guessed by AI.
  • Honest Refusal Engine: Halts and explains why when CRS mismatches, spatial overlap is <80%, or cloud cover is >80% without SAR.
  • Visual Proof & Vector Export: Every answer ships with an interactive agreement map and QGIS-compliant GeoJSON layers.
💡 Innovation & Uniqueness
  • Elevation-Gated Radar: Uses terrain slope and aspect from DEMs to prevent radar mountain shadows from being misclassified as floodwater.
  • Actionable Disagreement Maps: Highlights exactly where optical and radar evidence conflict under broken cloud cover.
  • Deterministic Math Contract: Physical quantities (hectares flooded, NDVI vegetation index) are computed by deterministic C-libraries.
  • Offline Tactical Field Packs: Compact packages (<10 MB) containing maps, vectors, and PDF dossiers usable by field rescue teams without internet.

⚙️ Robust Engineering Architecture
  • Strict Optical/SAR Separation: Radar backscatter is mathematically quarantined to deterministic signal processing tools; optical VLM never processes raw SAR imagery.
  • Linear Power Processing: Backscatter conversion $\sigma^0_{\text{lin}} = 10^{(\sigma^0_{\text{dB}}/10)}$ guarantees that cross-polarization ratios ($\text{VV}/\text{VH}$) are never calculated via invalid dB subtraction.
  • Interactive Feedback Loop: Field officers can issue refinement queries ("Focus on Nagaon highway corridor", "Tune threshold to -14 dB") directly updating the active GeoJSON layer.

Feasibility, Viability & Risk Mitigation

Scientifically defensible, cost-effective, and engineered for air-gapped national deployment

TCO Optimization Sovereign Air-Gapped Security
FEASIBILITY
  • FOSS Tooling: Built entirely on open-source libraries (FastAPI, Rasterio, GDAL, PyTorch) with zero recurring licensing fees.
  • Public Satellite Feeds: Direct ingestion of ISRO Bhuvan GeoAI, Copernicus Sentinel, and USGS imagery streams.
  • CPU Emergency Fallback: Runs on standard field laptops without requiring expensive GPU clusters during disaster blackouts.
CHALLENGES & RISKS
  • Cloud Cover: Optical scenes blocked by monsoon weather.
  • Radar Shadow: Mountain backslopes mimic specular reflection.
  • Temporal Misalignment: Date differences cause false change detection.
  • Data Volume: Multi-gigabyte GeoTIFFs cause latency.
VIABILITY & TCO
  • Economic ROI: Saves ₹15–20 Lakhs/yr in proprietary GIS server licensing (ArcGIS) for state disaster agencies.
  • Air-Gapped Security: Deploys 100% offline on-premise; sensitive defense/ISRO imagery never leaves sovereign infrastructure.
  • Scalability: Easily extensible from flood mapping to cyclone impact, agricultural crop damage, and forest fires.
MITIGATION STRATEGIES
  • Multi-Sensor Fusion: SAR microwave penetrates 100% of clouds.
  • Elevation Gating: SRTM/CartoDEM slope filters eliminate false alarms.
  • Affine Co-Registration: Sub-pixel geometric alignment before change calculation.
  • Tiled Streaming: COG (Cloud Optimized GeoTIFF) windowed window reads.
